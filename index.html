Terrance Credit Card Float App

Cards, bills, utilization alerts & monthly “which card pays which bill” planner.

Cards

Enter up to 15 cards (limit, balance, days). These mirror your “Credit Cards” sheet.

Bills

Enter monthly bills and assign to a card. These mirror your “Bill Master List”.

Utilization Alerts

Shows utilization %, zone (Safe/Warning/Caution/Danger), and room to 30% / 10%.

Monthly Plan – Which Card Pays Which Bill

Sorted by bill due day. This is your “which card pays what” view.

Per-Card Payment Summary

For each card: bills assigned, utilization after charges, “Pay THIS” amount to target 10% utilization.
